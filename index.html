about

WHAT IS
PROJECT NEXUS?

"Innovation challenge for the next-us."

Project Nexus 2026 is an AI-focused innovation challenge organized by IEEE Multimedia University Student Branch and co-organized by Engineering Society MMU, IEM MMU, Engineers Without Borders MMU, IEEE MMU PES, and UTP E3S2, which includes a series of workshops and an Innovation Challenge that happens in multiple phases. This event will be opened to all higher learning institutions in Malaysia and aims to encourage AI-assisted and real-world problem-solving across various technology tracks.

Expect a unique journey from an intensive Mentorship Phase to an exciting Build Phase culminating in a mandatory working hardware prototype, and an exciting Grand Finale where your system goes live in an exhibition.

Exciting prizes up for grabs with a total prize pool of RM10,000! This is the perfect opportunity to rise up to the occasion and demonstrate your skills within this field.

Join us at Project Nexus 2026 for a journey of innovation, problem-solving, and networking. Get ready to build your prototype and aim for the top!

judging criteria — phase 1

HOW PROPOSALS
ARE EVALUATED
(PHASE 1)

Phase 1 shortlists 10 teams per track based on your written proposal and 3-minute video pitch.

25%
Problem Identification

Identify a critical real-world problem with clear benefit if solved.

20%
Conceptual Innovation

A unique yet efficient solution that stands out from conventional approaches.

20%
Integration Strategy

A clear, logical plan showing how your solution integrates into the problem.

15%
System Design

Visual designs, system diagrams, and clear architecture graphs.

10%
Improvement Plan

Future growth potential and a clear roadmap for scaling the solution.

10%
Team Diversity

Diversity across gender, race, university, and faculty (including WIE).

Project Nexus 2026 is a hybrid event. Phase 1 submissions (proposals and pitch videos) are submitted online. The Grand Finale is a physical on-site exhibition held at Multimedia University, Cyberjaya.

Each team must have between 2 to 4 members. At least 1 member must identify as female. Solo participation is not permitted.
